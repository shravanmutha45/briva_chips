Farm to Bag Journey

HOW REAL FRUIT TURNS INTO SERIOUS CRUNCH

From the mist-clad groves of Wayanad to your snacking stash in four transparent artisanal steps.

01

Pick Ripe Kerala Jackfruit

Directly harvested from smallholder farm collectives across Wayanad and Idukki when the bulbs emit their sweet tropical scent.

02

Hand-Deshucked & Grain Slicing

Each bulb is manually depitted and sliced along its natural longitudinal fibers to guarantee optimal tensile snapping crunch.

03

Low-Temp Vacuum Fry (<80°C)

Sealed in negative pressure vessels where moisture boils at gentle warm temperatures, leaving fruit fibers golden rather than charred.

04

Centrifuge & Nitrogen Flush

High-speed centrifugal de-oiling removes surface fats. Sealed inside high-barrier foil bags under nitrogen to preserve maximum crunch.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, vacuum science, and shipping.

Is it really fried? How does vacuum frying work? expand_more

Yes, but in an entirely non-traditional way. Vacuum frying occurs in closed low-pressure chambers where water boils at just 60°C to 80°C instead of 180°C. This allows fruit moisture to evaporate rapidly without scorching, resulting in 70% less oil absorption and zero acrylamide build-up.

Does it contain added sugar or artificial sweeteners? expand_more

None whatsoever! We exclusively use ripe Varikka jackfruit naturally loaded with floral tropical fructose. There is zero sucrose, zero maltodextrin, zero stevia, and zero artificial flavors added.

What is the shelf life and how should I store it? expand_more

BRIVA chips have a sealed shelf life of 6 months from packaging. Each pouch features an airtight zip-lock mechanism. For optimal crispness, reseal immediately after snacking and store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

How fast is shipping across India? expand_more

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours via express air logistics. Metro locations (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad) receive delivery within 2 to 3 working days. Non-metro cities take 3 to 5 business days. Free shipping applies automatically to all orders above ₹300!
